ABOUT US

Welcome to A.S. Model Public School

Welcome to A.S. Model Public School, a nurturing and progressive co-educational institution located in Banbirpurkuri, Uttar Pradesh. We offer a high-quality education from Playway to Class VIII, preparing students for academic excellence and lifelong learning.

Our educational approach focuses on developing students academically, personally and socially, while providing an environment that encourages curiosity, creativity, critical thinking and strong moral values.

Preparing Students for a Successful Future
