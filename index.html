ABOUT US

Welcome to A.S. Model School

Welcome to A.S. Model Public School, a nurturing and progressive co-educational institution located in Banbirpurkuri, Uttar Pradesh. We offer a high-quality education from Playway to Class 12th, preparing students for academic excellence and lifelong success.

Our educational approach focuses on developing students academically, personally and socially, while providing an environment that encourages curiosity, creativity, critical thinking and strong moral values.

OUR MISSION

Preparing Students for a Successful Future

01

03

04

Prepare students for success in higher education and their chosen careers.
